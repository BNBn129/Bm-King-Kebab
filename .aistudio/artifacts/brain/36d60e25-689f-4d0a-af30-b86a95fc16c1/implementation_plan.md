# Complete Refurbishment & Reimagination: BM King Kebab

### 1. Root Cause Analysis of Previous Dissatisfaction
- **Navbar Clutter**: Desktop menu displayed cluttered horizontal navigation items (`O Nas`, `Galerie`, `Menu`, etc.) contrary to the requirement where the three-bar hamburger menu in the top right handles navigation.
- **Hero on Mobile**: Previous versions placed food pictures on mobile screens, squeezing the headline into a small, awkward space with generic badge icons.
- **Fragmented Subpage Architecture**: Navigating swapped the entire view into isolated tab subpages with breadcrumbs, breaking the natural fluid flow of a real restaurant website.

### 2. Fundamental Architectural & Visual Refurbishment
- **Clean Master 3-Bar Navigation**:
  - Eliminated horizontal desktop links.
  - Left: Refined brand mark (`BM KING KEBAB • Łódź · Sacharowa 45A`).
  - Right: Live status indicator (`🟢 Otwarte do 01:00`), direct phone dial button, interactive order/cart trigger with counter, and the master 3-bar hamburger button.
  - Hamburger Drawer: Full-height slide-over drawer with smooth animations, sections jump links, direct Google Maps link, Pyszne.pl button, and operating hours.
- **Monumental Editorial Hero**:
  - Deep warm espresso/charcoal background (`#141210`) with ambient amber warmth.
  - Mobile screens: **Zero cheap food pictures**. Prominent, authoritative, bold editorial headline commanding the viewport:
    - *RZEMIEŚLNICZY KEBAB Z OGNIA I PIECA*
    - Clear, welcoming prose and high-contrast call-to-actions.
    - Live trust bar: Orły Gastronomii 2025 & 2026, Google 4.9★ (928+ opinii), free Turkish çay for guests.
  - Desktop screens: Elegant cinematic framed visual showcase on the right.
- **Culinary Commitments Strip**:
  - Freshly baked pita straight from the oven.
  - 100% selected meats marinated in Anatolian herbs.
  - Morning-fresh vegetables and daily homemade sauces.
  - Complimentary traditional Turkish çay for every guest.
- **Unified, Cohesive Restaurant Experience**:
  - Complete interactive menu with sticky category pills (Tortille, Talerze, Boxy, Pita, Burgery, Wege, Dodatki).
  - Search bar and dietary filters.
  - Customizer modal for meat selection, sauce pairing, and extras.
  - Story & Orły Gastronomii accreditation section.
  - Verified Google guest reviews.
  - Location, interactive opening hours table, and embedded Google Maps.
  - Sticky mobile bottom bar for 1-tap phone calls and order review.
